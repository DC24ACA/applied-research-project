## Limitations
* LLMs may produce convincing explanations even when conclusions are not fully supported by the available evidence.
* important events can be missed, incorrectly interpreted or connected to the wrong activity.
* incomplete or noisy security evidence can reduce the quality of the final investigation.
* stronger reasoning does not automatically guarantee that an investigation is accurate, complete or evidence-supported.

## Important point
* errors become more important as the level of human oversight decreases because there is less opportunity for incorrect findings to be corrected.


* In brief – the main concern is not whether an LLM can produce a convincing investigation report but whether each conclusion can be traced back to the evidence provided.

* Link to project – these limitations support evaluating each LLM for accuracy, completeness and evidence support against independent ground truth.
