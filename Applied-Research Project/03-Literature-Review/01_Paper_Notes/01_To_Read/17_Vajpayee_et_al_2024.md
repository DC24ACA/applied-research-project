
# Chat GPT and Cyber Risks: Need of Custom Large Language Models (LLMs)

## Bibliographic Information

Citation: Vajpayee, P., Karuppiah, C. and Hossain, G. (2024). Chat GPT and cyber risks: Need of custom large language models (LLMs). *2024 IEEE 3rd International Conference on Data, Decision and Systems (ICDDS).* doi: 10.1109/ICDDS62937.2024.10910783.

Supporting Literature

Status: Read 

## Research Aim

* Compare cybersecurity-related responses from different LLMs.
* Examine security risks associated with inappropriate or overly detailed LLM responses.

## Methodology

* Tested ChatGPT 3.5, Llama 2 and Mistral.
* Asked each model the same cybersecurity-related questions.
* Compared responses for potentially risky information and inconsistencies.

## Key Findings

* Different LLMs produced noticeably different cybersecurity responses.
* Some responses exposed information that could potentially assist attackers.
* The authors argue that customised LLMs may reduce organisational security risks. 

## Strengths

* Directly compares several LLMs using the same questions.
* Highlights differences and inconsistencies between LLM outputs.

## Limitations Relevant to My Scope

* Uses a small set of cybersecurity questions rather than security logs.
* Does not evaluate incident investigation or evidence reconstruction.
* Uses older LLM versions.

## Relevance to My Project

* Supports comparing multiple LLMs under consistent conditions.
* Shows that different LLMs can produce different conclusions from identical inputs.

## Methodology I Could Adapt

* Provide identical prompts to each LLM.
* Compare output differences between models.
* Use predefined criteria to evaluate responses.

## Research Gap

* Does not provide security evidence or logs for analysis.
* Does not test autonomous incident investigation.
* Does not compare LLM conclusions against known attack ground truth.
